FAQ

Quick answers to common questions.

Quick answers to the questions people ask us most.

What do I need to rent a car?

A valid driving license and a government ID. Some cars also need a deposit card.

How old do I need to be?

You must be at least 21 years old. For sports cars, the age is 25.

How do I book a car?

Pick a car on our Cars page, press Rent Now, and fill in the form. We will call you to confirm.

How much is the deposit?

The deposit depends on the car. It is returned to you after you bring the car back in good shape.

Can I cancel my booking?

Yes. You can cancel up to 24 hours before pickup and get your money back.

Is insurance included?

Basic insurance is included in every price. You can add full cover for extra peace of mind.

Is fuel included?

No. The car comes with a full tank and should be returned with a full tank.

Is there a limit on how far I can drive?

Most rentals include 200 km a day. Extra kilometers have a small fee. Ask us about unlimited plans.

Can someone else drive the car?

Yes. Add them as an extra driver when you book. They must also show a valid license.

Do you offer airport pickup?

Yes. Tell us your flight time and we will meet you at the airport with your keys.

Can I rent a child seat or GPS?

Yes. Both are available for a small daily fee. Please ask when you book.

What if the car breaks down?

Call our 24-hour help line right away. We will fix the problem or send you another car.

What if I return the car late?

Please call us first. A short delay may be free. Longer delays are charged by the hour.

Which payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, debit cards, and credit cards.

Can I take the car outside the city?

Yes, within the country. For trips abroad, please talk to us first so we can plan together.
