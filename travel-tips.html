Travel Tips

10 simple tips for a safe and happy trip.

Small habits make a big difference. Here are our 10 best tips for a safe and happy trip.

01

Check the car first

Walk around the car before you drive. Take photos of any scratches, so there are no surprises later.

02

Check the tires and lights

Look at the tires, headlights, and brake lights. Ask us for help if something looks wrong.

03

Plan your route

Use a map app and save your route before you leave. Signal can be weak in the mountains.

04

Pack water and snacks

A long drive can be tiring. Keep water, snacks, and a small first-aid kit in the car.

05

Take breaks often

Stop every two hours to stretch and rest. A tired driver is not a safe driver.

06

Fill up the tank early

Do not wait for the last bar. Fuel up when you pass a station, especially on empty roads.

07

Know the road rules

Speed limits and signs change from place to place. Read them and stay calm.

08

Keep your papers close

Keep your license, ID, and rental papers in one safe place, easy to reach.

09

Drive safe in rain and night

Slow down, keep more space, and turn on your lights. Your safety comes first.

10

Return the car on time

Plan to arrive a little early. It helps you avoid extra fees and stress.
